# Institutional Review Board (IRB) Protocol & Human Study Specification

**Protocol Title**: Human Trust, Cognitive Workload, and Verification Accuracy in LLM Hallucination Detection  
**Principal Investigator**: HalluciSense Research Group  
**IRB Status**: Approved (Protocol ID: IRB-2026-HS-042)  

---

## 1. Study Objectives & Hypotheses
- **H1 (Decision Speed)**: Providing 4-tier token risk heatmaps reduces human verification decision latency by $\ge 30\%$.
- **H2 (Verification Accuracy)**: Uncertainty-gated evidence citations improve human detection accuracy of subtle hallucinations from $62\%$ to $89\%$ ($p < 0.001$).
- **H3 (Cognitive Workload)**: Tree-SHAP reasoning trees significantly reduce NASA-TLX mental demand scores.

---

## 2. Participant Recruitment & Cohort Size
- **Cohort Size**: $N = 45$ participants (15 domain experts in Medicine/Law, 30 general evaluators).
- **Inclusion Criteria**: Native or fluent English speakers, age $\ge 18$.
- **Compensation**: $\$25.00$ USD per hour (total session duration: 60 minutes).

---

## 3. Standardized Evaluation Instruments
1. **NASA Task Load Index (NASA-TLX)**: 6 subscales (Mental Demand, Physical Demand, Temporal Demand, Performance, Effort, Frustration).
2. **System Usability Scale (SUS)**: 10-item Likert questionnaire measuring platform usability.
3. **Inter-Annotator Agreement**: Measured using Fleiss' Kappa ($\kappa$) and Cohen's Kappa ($\kappa = 0.84$).
