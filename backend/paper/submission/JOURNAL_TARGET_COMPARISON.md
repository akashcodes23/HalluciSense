## 2. Strategic Journal Targeting Recommendation
1. **Primary Target:** *Information Fusion* (Elsevier) — Due to the central contribution of availability-aware dynamic indicator masking ($\mathbf{m} \in \{0, 1\}^3$) and reliability modulation ($\mathbf{r}$).
2. **Alternative Target 1:** *Knowledge-Based Systems* (Elsevier) — Due to the knowledge-grounding and NLI reasoning focus.
3. **Alternative Target 2:** *Expert Systems with Applications* (Elsevier) — Due to the end-to-end pipeline implementation and memory safety engineering.
