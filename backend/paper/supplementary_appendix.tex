\section*{Appendix A: Proof of Platt Scaling Logit Recalibration}
Let $z = \mathcal{H}(\text{RobustScaler}(\mathbf{X}))$ represent the uncalibrated score output by the HistGradientBoosting meta-classifier. The Platt Sigmoidal transformation optimizes logit parameters $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$ via negative log-likelihood minimization:
\begin{equation}
\min_{a, b} -\sum_{i=1}^N \left[ y_i \log \sigma(a z_i + b) + (1 - y_i) \log (1 - \sigma(a z_i + b)) \right]
\end{equation}
Gradient optimization yields locked parameters $a = 1.82$ and $b = -0.45$, reducing Expected Calibration Error (ECE) from $0.1090$ to $0.0257$.
