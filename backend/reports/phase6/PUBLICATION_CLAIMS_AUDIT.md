## 3. Mandatory Publication Disclaimers
1. **Offline Evaluation vs. Live Interactive Evaluation**: Clearly demarcate that offline batch benchmarks test **Pillar 1 Evidence Grounding**, whereas live streaming sessions test **Full Three-Pillar Adaptive Fusion**.
2. **Dataset Provenance**: Explicitly state that the $N=750$ corpus is a **curated multi-domain diagnostic suite ($15 \times 50$)** with deterministic seed 42.
