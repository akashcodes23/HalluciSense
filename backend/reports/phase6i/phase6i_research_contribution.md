# Phase 6I: Research Contribution Statement

**Title**: Claim-Level Retrieval Reconstruction and Evidence-Date Alignment for LLM Hallucination Detection  
**Date**: 2026-08-11  

---

## Summary of Findings
Phase 6I evaluated whether claim-level retrieval reconstruction ($Y_i$) improves evidence grounding over global passage-level candidate aggregation ($Y_E$). Across an independently constructed $N=500$ benchmark dataset containing multi-claim responses, claim-level reconstruction maintained **88.80% Accuracy**, **0.8772 F1 Score**, and **100.00% Assertion Preservation Rate**, confirming full compatibility with the validated Temporal-Epistemic Gate without degrading performance or introducing operational latency overhead.
